    FAQ

    Are your products authentic?

    Yes. NOIR only sells authentic, carefully selected pieces. If you have any doubt about an item, message us and we'll answer honestly.

    How do I order?

    Tap any ORDER button or message us on WhatsApp with the item you want. We confirm price and availability, then arrange payment and delivery.

    Mobile Money or bank transfer. For pre-orders, we ask for a 50% deposit and you pay the balance on delivery.

    If an item arrives damaged or wrong, message us within 48 hours and we'll make it right. Opened perfumes and skincare cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.
